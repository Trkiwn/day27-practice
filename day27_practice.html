Five short questions, five different formats. These test whether you can correctly read a prompt before you ever start writing.

Intermediate

Fix, Translate, and Construct

~23 min

Now you diagnose broken introductions, translate target phrasing, and build your own sentences.

1.Find the mistake in this introduction, then rewrite it correctly.Error Correction
"Some people say that online learning is just as effective as traditional classroom learning, while others disagree. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."
2.Find the mistake in this introduction, then rewrite it correctly.Error Correction
"Retirement age is an important issue affecting many countries around the world today."
3.Translate each sentence into English, using the target phrase given in brackets.Translation

1. Nhiều người tin rằng con người nên giảm tiêu thụ thịt để bảo vệ môi trường. (a widely held belief)

2. Một số người cho rằng độ tuổi nghỉ hưu nên được nâng lên vì tuổi thọ con người đang tăng. (should be raised)

3. Tôi hoàn toàn không đồng ý với quan điểm này. (I completely disagree)

4. Theo quan điểm của tôi, cả hai khía cạnh đều có giá trị riêng. (both sides have merit)

4.Given Sentence 1 below, write a matching Sentence 2 using a Partly Agree position.Sentence Writing
"It is often suggested that students should be free to choose between online and classroom-based learning."
5.Paraphrase the topic statement below into your own Sentence 1. You must use at least one word-form swap (e.g. a verb changed to a noun).Sentence Writing
Original: "Governments should increase taxes on fast food to reduce obesity rates."
